World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

US, Russian and Ukrainian negotiators held a second day of talks in Abu Dhabi, the first three-way meeting since February, led for the US by envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff; a prisoner exchange was discussed (Reuters). Russia said it shot down 92 drones over the Leningrad region overnight.

Leaders opened the COP31 climate summit's leaders' meeting in Antalya, Turkey, with more than 100 leaders from 95 countries expected; President Erdogan, UN chief Antonio Guterres and COP31 president Murat Kurum spoke at the opening (Reuters). The US, which quit the Paris Agreement in January, is not taking part.

In Washington, the House voted 211-204 to send impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, blocking an immediate vote; Republican Thomas Massie brought the charges over the Iran war, and was the only Republican to vote against the referral (Reuters). Democrats are expected to revive the effort in January.

Northwestern Polytechnical University, a Chinese university tied to the People's Liberation Army, bought two Nvidia H200 AI chips for 874,700 yuan ($123,000) despite a US export blacklist, procurement records show; Reuters first reported the purchases.

Pakistan's former prime minister Imran Khan was taken to hospital for treatment and returned to prison, sources said (Reuters).
