# Research notes — world news of Wednesday 11 November 2026 (verified 12 Nov; dropped items at foot)

1. **Abu Dhabi (AP, DPA):** Two days of US-mediated Russia–Ukraine talks, first trilateral meeting since February, ended with no ceasefire/territory breakthrough; both sides agreed in principle to a large POW exchange — Kushner: the biggest humanitarian release yet, numbers finalised Thursday; Zelenskyy floated 1,000–1,500 per side, Russian General Staff said 1,000+ each within 2–3 weeks; Medinsky and Umerov confirmed. Kushner and Witkoff go to Moscow (Putin) Thursday, then Dublin (Zelenskyy).
2. **Hormuz (Reuters):** Parliament speaker Ghalibaf: the strait will never return to its pre-war status; Tehran awaits a US answer to its seven conditions. UKMTO reported another tanker incident Wednesday; Iran said a tanker caught fire after hitting a naval mine — Reuters could not verify. At least 10 ships hit in November's first 10 days, after 37 attacks in October (Washington Institute); IMO: 133 incidents since late February, 25+ seafarers killed.
3. **COP31, Antalya (Reuters, UN News, AFP):** Two-day leaders' summit opened Wed; 100+ leaders expected; Erdogan opened, Guterres and COP31 president Murat Kurum spoke. Guterres: "make or bake time", >2.5C warming even if current plans are met, urged taxes on fossil-fuel firms. Turkey's 35-by-35 pledge: electricity from ~a fifth to 35% of world energy by 2035. US absent (quit Paris in January, second year out). COP31 runs 9–20 Nov.
4. **Iran-backed plots (AFP, BBC, DOJ):** Eight-count indictment unsealed in Manhattan charges al-Saadi (32, Iranian-Iraqi, jailed in New York), Mohajeri (48) and al-Hassani (35) as operatives for the IRGC and Kataib Hezbollah over nearly 20 attacks/plots in the US and Europe, incl. the September RAF Fairford plot (fuel-laden vans, 26–27 Sept); other two not in US custody; Iran denied involvement. Separately, Treasury Secretary Bessent was moved to a military base after Iran-attributed death threats (NYT/Fox).
5. **US soldier arrested (BBC, Met Police):** A 41-year-old senior US Army soldier was arrested near Gloucester under sections 1 and 3 of the National Security Act 2023 on suspicion of spying for Russia; based at Imjin Barracks, Innsworth — HQ of NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps (BBC); held in London, unnamed, uncharged. The Kremlin denied involvement on Wednesday.
6. **Spain strike (Reuters):** First general strike in 14 years, over housing, under three weeks before the 29 November snap election; backed by UGT, CCOO and tenants' groups; unions said ~4 million workers took part. Housing deficit ~750,000 homes (central bank); average rent 1,176 euros = ~98.7% of a young person's monthly salary (Youth Council); power demand fell up to 15.5% (Red Electrica); ~5% of flights cancelled (AENA).
7. **Mississippi (AP, CBS):** AP called the Senate race for Democrat Scott Colom on 11 Nov after a week of counting; Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith conceded. At the call: Colom 473,336 (49.19%), Hyde-Smith 468,667 (48.71%), margin 4,669 votes. Colom is Mississippi's first Black senator since Reconstruction; counties certify 13 Nov.
8. **Hegseth (Reuters):** The House voted 211–204 to refer Thomas Massie's impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, blocking an immediate vote and effectively ending the effort this Congress; Massie, who brought the charges over the Iran war, was the only Republican to vote against the referral.
9. **Congo Ebola (Reuters):** Confirmed cases surpassed 11,000 with more than 5,300 deaths (government data); outbreak declared mid-May, caused by the Bundibugyo species — no approved vaccine or treatment — and is the second-worst Ebola outbreak on record at six months.
10. **Haiti (BBC):** Bukele said Salvadoran troops backing Haitian police destroyed the 400 Mawozo gang's HQ and leader Lanmo San Jou's (Wilson Joseph) house in Croix-des-Bouquets in a ground/air assault; the BBC could not verify his video or that it was the HQ; reported toll of up to 20 killed is unconfirmed. Haiti's PM Fils-Aime praised it. 2,000+ Salvadoran troops deployed since late October; the strike was Sunday 8 Nov, announced Tuesday 10 Nov, reported by the BBC on 11 Nov.
11. **Nvidia (Reuters exclusive, 11 Nov):** Northwestern Polytechnical University (Xi'an; close PLA ties; on the US Entity List since 2001) bought two Nvidia H200 chips on 21 Oct for 874,700 yuan (~$123,000) via a Chinese supplier, procurement records show; it also rented restricted-chip capacity (five contracts this year), and Reuters found 26 records of Chinese institutions obtaining restricted Nvidia processors or access via domestic suppliers. No comment from Nvidia, the university or Commerce.
12. **Markets (Reuters):** US October CPI (released Tuesday): +0.4% m/m (Sept +0.6%) vs 0.3% forecast; core +0.3% vs 0.2%; no y/y rate (Oct 2025 shutdown left no base). Asian shares fell Wednesday; the 10-year Treasury yield rose to ~5%; traders fully priced a quarter-point Fed hike for 8–9 December.
